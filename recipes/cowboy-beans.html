Cowboy Beans Recipes

These cowboy beans, baked beans with a meaty twist, are full of simple ingredients and hearty flavor. Serve as a hearty side dish (inspiration below), or as a main with a side of cornbread to soak up every last bite.

Cowbow Bean Ingedients

  • Meat: Bacon and ground beef take regular baked beans up a notch.
  • Beans: You'll need canned baked beans with pork, kidney beans, and lima beans.
  • Onions: Two diced onions add tons of flavor.
  • Brown sugar: Use store brought or homemade ketchup.
  • Mustard:A teaspoon of prepared mustard lends an extra depth of flavor.

Ingredients

  • 1 pound ground beef
  • ½ pound bacon, chopped
  • 1 can baked beans with pork
  • 1 can kidney beans, rinsed and drained
  • 2 onions, chopped
  • 1 cup ketchup
  • 1 cup brown sugar
  • 1 teaspoon prepared mustard

Direction

  1. Gather the ingredients.
  2. Heat a large skillet over medium-high heat. Cook and stir ground beef and bacon together in the hot skillet until beef is browned and crumbly, about 7 to 10 minutes.
  3. Transfer hamburger and bacon into a slow cooker; stir in baked beans with pork, kidney beans, lima beans, onions, ketchup, brown sugar, and mustard.
  4. Cover and cook on Low until thickened and bubbling, at least 3 hours.
